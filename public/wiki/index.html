RaceGPT
Player guide

RaceGPT is a time-attack driving game in your browser. Drive from start to finish on any of four tracks, then try to improve your time. Ghosts show your personal best and a built-in reference run.

01

Start your first run

  1. Choose a track. Test Track A is selected by default. All four tracks are available immediately; choosing another reloads the game on that track.
  2. Select Start Run. The car stays at the start through a three-second countdown. The timer begins at GO.
  3. Follow the road to the finish. Checkpoint gates record your elapsed time along the way. The goal is to finish as quickly as you can.
  4. Run again. The first completed player run becomes your local best. A faster finish replaces it and its ghost; a slower finish leaves it intact.

Try short steering inputs and brake before tighter turns. Use R to recover from a mistake, or Enter for a fresh timed attempt.

Keep holding the brake at low speed to reverse. Space uses the same brake input; it is not a separate handbrake.

In menus, use Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and arrow keys to adjust sliders. Starting or resuming a run makes the driving keys available immediately.

Gamepad

Use the left stick to steer, the right trigger for gas and the left trigger for brake. A / Cross resets the checkpoint and confirms from the menu or finish screen; B / Circle restarts; Start / Options pauses or resumes. These are the usual labels for standard controller buttons 0, 1 and 9. Your browser's mapping may differ, and the game uses the first connected controller.

Touch & mobile controls

Touch controls appear on devices with a coarse pointer, such as a touchscreen, or when the browser window is 780 px wide or less. During a run, steering is at the bottom left, Gas and Brake at the bottom right, and Reset and Pause at the top right.

Left / right arrows
Hold to steer. Holding both arrows cancels touch steering.
Gas / Brake
Hold to accelerate or brake. You can use separate fingers for simultaneous steering, Gas and Brake. Keep holding Brake at low speed to reverse.
Reset
Press for the same checkpoint recovery as R. Two quick presses use the double-reset behavior.
Pause
Press to open the pause menu. A second finger can press Pause while another finger holds a driving control.

Lifting a finger releases its control, as does an interrupted touch. Opening a menu, switching away, resizing or rotating the screen clears held keyboard and touch inputs. Press the driving controls again after rotating or resuming.

Pause before switching away: leaving the game window releases held inputs but does not automatically pause the run timer.

Menus on a phone

Tap a track, then Start Run. Menus scroll in portrait and landscape, so you can reach Settings and Return to Arcade even on a short screen. At the finish, choose Run Again or Main Menu.

From the pause menu, Resume continues the attempt and Restart Run begins a fresh countdown. Settings opens the options, Main Menu leaves the attempt while keeping saved bests, and Return to Arcade exits the game.

Using more than one input device

Active gamepad steering takes priority over touch, which takes priority over keyboard steering. Gas and brake each use the strongest input from the devices.

04

Car handling

You drive one fixed car with seven automatic forward gears. The speedometer shows km/h and the current gear. There is no car selection, tuning or manual gear control.

Steering & drift

Steering becomes less responsive at high speed and has no effect while stationary. Braking while steering at speed reduces sideways grip, allowing the car to drift. Drifting does not earn points.

Road & barriers

Driving off the road slows the car. Barriers along supported road edges can push it back or deflect it, costing speed. Collisions do not cause damage.

Jumps & landings

The car can become airborne from ramps or road gaps. Steering in the air is weaker, and hard landings reduce speed. If you miss the road, use a checkpoint reset to recover.

Cornering tips

Brake earlier when a bend catches you out, then release the brake before you unintentionally reverse. Keep the car near the road when crossing timing gates.

05

Timing & resets

3 · 2 · 1Countdown→ GOTimer starts→ 1–2 gatesSplit recorded→ FinishTime compared
Current
Elapsed simulation time for this attempt. The timer stops while paused or in Settings.
Best
Your fastest saved time on the selected track. Dashes mean there is no saved best.
Split
Total elapsed time at the latest checkpoint crossed. It is not a sector duration or a difference from your best.

Times are displayed as minutes:seconds.milliseconds. Cross checkpoint gates forward, in route order, and near the road to record a split. Crossing the finish forward near the road ends the attempt.

Checkpoint reset versus full restart

  • Press R once: restore the car's position, direction and velocity from roughly half a second before the last checkpoint you passed. Before any checkpoint, reset to the start.
  • Press R twice quickly: two presses within 360 milliseconds, after a checkpoint, place the car just beyond that checkpoint with zero velocity.
  • The timer keeps going: a checkpoint reset never rewinds elapsed time. Driving input is locked for 0.18 seconds after a reset, but no fixed time penalty is added.
  • Press Enter for a fresh run: clear the current attempt's time, splits and recording, return to the start, and begin another countdown. Your saved best stays intact.

The game does not require every checkpoint to have been passed before you finish. Checkpoints record splits and give you recovery points; they do not enforce a complete route.

06

Ghosts & personal bests

Your Best replays your fastest saved run on the selected track. It appears once you have a saved recording; the "Your best ghost" setting shows or hides it.

Codex 5.5 is the built-in reference driver. Its ghost replays the run used for the track's "MODEL PBS" billboard. The "Model ghosts" setting shows or hides this ghost. Neither ghost collides with your car.

A finish faster than the model reference reports how much you beat its PB by. Your own best is replaced only by a strictly faster player run. There are no unlocks, currency, medals or online player rankings in this version.

07

Settings & saves

Open Settings from the main menu or pause menu. Changes are saved as you make them.

Audio volume
Adjust from silent to full volume. The default is 65%. Start or resume a run to activate browser audio.
Your best ghost
Show or hide your saved player ghost. On by default; hiding it does not delete the recording.
Model ghosts
Show or hide the built-in reference ghost. On by default; its PB comparison remains available at the finish.
Input overlay
Show steering, gas, brake and input-source readouts during the run. Off by default; I also toggles it.

Settings, your selected track and one personal best with its ghost per track are saved in your browser. Returning to the menu, restarting or changing tracks keeps those bests.

Keep your saves: use the same browser profile and site address. Records do not sync between devices or browsers, and there is no save import, export or cloud backup. Clearing site data removes your records; private browsing may discard them when the session ends. A local copy of the game has separate saves from the public site.

If browser storage is blocked or full, saves can fail without an error message. Opening this guide does not change game saves.

Where Return to Arcade takes you

The exit button normally returns to the Arcade. A game link can supply a custom return address, including another website. Valid destinations may be relative or use HTTP(S), but cannot contain embedded login credentials. Malformed addresses and other schemes fall back to the Arcade.

08

Current limits

  • Time attack only. There is no combat, multiplayer race, campaign, unlock tree or vehicle upgrade system.
  • Local records only. The model billboard is a built-in reference, not an online player leaderboard.
  • Simulation timing. The clock advances with physics steps rather than directly with wall-clock time. Very slow frames can make an attempt diverge from real elapsed time.
  • Device support. Touch controls and phone menus were checked in Chrome emulation. Physical phones, iOS Safari, hardware gamepads and sustained mobile performance remain untested. There is no tilt steering.
  • Graphics support. The game requires WebGL. This guide works without JavaScript.
